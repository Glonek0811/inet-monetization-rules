Arma Reforger — Server Monetization Rules

Server: INET #1 | CONFLICT | POLSKA | PC/XBOX/PS5

1. General Information

INET operates a community of Arma Reforger servers focused on fair gameplay and providing stable multiplayer experiences. This monetization policy is intended to cover the current INET server and future servers operated by the INET community, including the planned INET Hardcore server.

2. Scope — Current and Future INET Servers

This proposal applies to the current INET server and to future Arma Reforger servers operated under the INET community, including the planned Hardcore server and any additional INET server projects.

Any monetization on these servers will follow the same cosmetic-only principles described in this policy and will be introduced only where permitted and after obtaining any approval required by Bohemia Interactive. No future INET server will use donations or subscriptions to provide gameplay advantages.

3. Purpose of Monetization

All donations and subscription payments are intended exclusively to cover the costs of operating and maintaining the server, including server hosting and technical infrastructure.

Support is entirely voluntary. The monetization system is not intended to generate personal profit.

4. Cosmetic Rewards

Players who voluntarily support the server through a one-time contribution or a recurring subscription may receive cosmetic patches for uniforms, vests, or backpacks.

These patches are visual customization items only. They do not provide gameplay advantages or unlock gameplay functionality.

5. Fair Gameplay

No payment or contribution will provide a competitive advantage. Cosmetic patches will not affect weapon performance, player statistics, equipment availability, vehicle access, or any other gameplay mechanics.

All players can access and play on the server on equal terms, whether or not they choose to support it financially.

6. Use of Proceeds

All proceeds received through the monetization system will be allocated exclusively to server hosting, infrastructure, and other necessary operating expenses.

Any surplus will be reserved for future server operating costs.

7. Voluntary Support and Subscriptions

Supporting the server is optional. Recurring subscriptions, if approved, may provide the same type of cosmetic-only reward described above.

Subscriptions will not grant priority access, privileged administration, exclusive gameplay features, or any gameplay advantage. Players will be able to manage or cancel recurring payments through the payment provider used for the contribution.

8. Compliance with Bohemia Interactive Rules

INET intends to comply with all applicable Bohemia Interactive monetization requirements. Monetized rewards and the proposed payment model will only be introduced after obtaining the required approval.

9. Contact

For questions about the community or its server policies, contact the INET administration through the INET Discord community.
